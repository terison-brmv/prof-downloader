# Privacy

Prof Downloader does not collect, store or send any personal data about you.

- **No accounts.** The app never asks you to sign in and does not use your accounts on any service.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.**
- **Everything stays on your Mac.** Links you paste, your download history and your settings are stored only on your computer. You can clear the history at any time in the app.
- **Network connections the app makes:**
  - to the platform of the link you pasted (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Threads) and its servers, to read the page and download the file you asked for;
  - to GitHub, to check for new versions of the app and of the download engine (yt-dlp). You can turn these checks off in Settings.
- **Nothing else.** The app has no server of its own and nobody receives your links or files.

Questions about privacy: open an [issue](../../issues).
